After running a query, results appear by collection with anonymised cohort
counts and a status showing progress from pending to complete on the HDR UK
Gateway. Select a collection name to open the linked page for that collection on
the Gateway.

Results show rounded cohort counts, not exact patient numbers. Low count
suppression is applied to protect identification, so ‘0’ may mean no results or
a suppressed low value. Suppression thresholds are set by each collection,
typically for counts below 10.

From the **Results** tab, you can edit or download your query.
